How to read the status

In OpenAlex — the article's DOI was found in OpenAlex.

Not found — the article was checked against OpenAlex but its DOI is not indexed there.

Not checked — the article has no DOI, or it could not be checked (e.g. the journal registers no DOIs, or the check was interrupted). This is different from "not found": it does not tell you whether the work is indexed, only that it wasn't verified.

Articles are matched by their registered DOI, so a "Not found" result can also occur when the DOI is registered with DataCite but has not (yet) been harvested by OpenAlex.
