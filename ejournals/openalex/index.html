Method & caveats

Works in OpenAlex is OpenAlex's own live count of works attributed to the journal's source (venue). For journals with no dedicated OpenAlex venue, it is the number of the journal's registered DOIs found in OpenAlex (usually attributed to the publisher, Universitäts- und Landesbibliothek Münster).

Works registered is the number of DOIs the journal registers with DataCite, as cached by the main dashboard's monthly crawl. This count is capped at 500 per journal, so journals showing exactly 500 may have more.

Coverage is Works in OpenAlex ÷ Works registered. A journal is marked missing when OpenAlex has neither a venue nor any of its works; partial when coverage is below 75%; and indexed when it has a venue with good coverage (or no DOI registration but a healthy venue).

Several journals' registered ISSNs differ from the ISSNs OpenAlex holds (or hold no ISSN at all), so matching is by exact title as well as ISSN.
