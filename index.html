Quantitative metrics are not a proxy for research quality. Publication and citation counts measure volume and attention, not significance, rigour, or societal value — read them as context, never as a verdict. This dashboard is a free and open-source alternative to commercial tools such as SciVal, built so that bibliometric transparency need not depend on a paid subscription.

Understanding OA types: Gold — published in a fully open-access journal. Green — freely available via a repository deposit. Hybrid — open under an APC in a subscription journal. Bronze — free to read on the publisher site without a stated licence. Closed — accessible only via subscription or paywall.

Methodology. OpenAlex does not expose Münster's internal faculty structure, so each faculty is approximated by its primary OpenAlex research field(s). Closely related faculties that share a field (e.g. the two theology faculties, or History/Philosophy and Philology under Arts & Humanities) will therefore show similar figures. Treat these as field-level proxies, not official faculty statistics.

About this monitor. The four Impact Areas (Dynamic Societies · Healthy Individuals · Conceptual Foundations & Emerging Technologies · Sustainable Futures) defined in the HEP are reconstructed here by grouping OpenAlex research fields. The partner-country panel highlights co-authorship with the Netherlands (University of Twente) and Brazil — both named as strategic partnerships in the plan — alongside other leading partners. Open-science and internationalisation figures are drawn from the Open Access and Collaboration sections above.
